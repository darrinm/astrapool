House rules

Arcade adds playful effects and a separate points score in every game. Pots earn 100; banks, kicks, combinations and multi-pots add bonuses. Consecutive scoring shots build a multiplier up to ×3. Floating points are pending until the shot settles; scratches and fouls void that shot's points. Racks won still decide the match. Choose Reduced effects for calmer motion. Free Play Cue and Fling have separate personal bests; changing modes during a rack makes it mixed practice.

Local 8-ball: take turns on this device. The table stays open after the break. Pocket only one group on a legal shot to claim solids or stripes. Clear your group, then call a pocket for the 8-ball.

Hit your group first, then pocket a ball or drive any ball to a cushion. Fouls give ball-in-hand anywhere. An early 8, a scratch with the 8, or the wrong pocket loses. An 8 on the break is spotted. A dry break needs four object balls to a cushion; otherwise the opponent breaks a fresh rack.

Vs Computer: Easy is forgiving and favors simple pots. Medium is accurate enough to build runs, but leaves openings. Hard plans ahead, uses spin and banks, and plays defensively when needed.
