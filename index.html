House rules

Arcade adds playful effects and a separate points score in every game. Pots earn 100; banks, kicks, combinations and multi-pots add bonuses. Consecutive scoring shots build a multiplier up to ×3. Floating points are pending until the shot settles; scratches and fouls void that shot's points. Racks won still decide the match. Choose Reduced effects for calmer motion. Free Play Cue and Fling have separate personal bests; changing modes during a rack makes it mixed practice.

Distinct bonuses stack before the streak multiplier. Each family pays its highest tier; a double kiss replaces the carom bonus for the same ball. Kicks pay once per shot. Only measured, contributing contacts count; cushion chatter and ambiguous collisions earn no trick bonus.

Local 8-ball: take turns on this device. The table stays open after the break. Pocket only one group on a legal shot to claim solids or stripes. Clear your group, then tap a highlighted pocket on the table to call the 8-ball.

Hit your group first, then pocket a ball or drive any ball to a cushion. Fouls give ball-in-hand anywhere. An early 8, a scratch with the 8, or the wrong pocket loses. An 8 on the break is spotted. A dry break needs four object balls to a cushion; otherwise the opponent breaks a fresh rack.

Vs Computer: Easy is forgiving and favors simple pots. Medium is accurate enough to build runs, but leaves openings. Hard plans ahead, uses spin and banks, and plays defensively when needed. Tricky prefers reliable banks, kicks, combinations and caroms, then plays for points among those choices. It takes a simple pot when the tricks are too risky and looks ahead for its next trick.
