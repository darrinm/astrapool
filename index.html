Pool house rules

Local 8-ball: take turns on this device. The table stays open after the break. Pocket only one group on a legal shot to claim solids or stripes. Clear your group, then call a pocket for the 8-ball.

Hit your group first, then pocket a ball or drive any ball to a cushion. Fouls give ball-in-hand anywhere. An early 8, a scratch with the 8, or the wrong pocket loses. An 8 on the break is spotted. A dry break needs four object balls to a cushion; otherwise the opponent breaks a fresh rack. Regular shots need no pocket call.

In Cue mode, pull back from the white ball and release to shoot. In Free Play’s Fling mode, grab any ball and throw it. Hold still to place it.
