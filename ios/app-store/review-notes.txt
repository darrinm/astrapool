Astra Pool is a complete 3D pool game. No account, sign-in, purchases, subscriptions, or advertising, or user chat are required.

Local play works offline: the game engine, sounds, planetary textures, and room environments are included in the app. Internet is needed for Play a Friend online matches and anonymous gameplay analytics. The app uses Apple's WebKit to run the shared game engine, with native sharing, invitation entry, and lifecycle/audio handling.

To play:
- Choose Vs Computer for a game against the AI, Local 8-ball for two players sharing one device, or Free Play for unrestricted practice.
- Drag from the cue ball to aim and set shot power, then release to shoot. Hold the aim steady briefly for finer control. Drag elsewhere to orbit; pinch to zoom.
- The top-right settings gear provides game modes, sound, AI difficulty, environments, Black Hole Gravity, Look Ahead, and Clairvoyant projected paths. Gravity is disabled for online matches.

To test online multiplayer without accounts:
1. Choose Play a Friend and create a private room.
2. Use Share invite to share the generated link with a second device. The link can be opened in a browser without installing the app.
3. To join from another installed copy, open settings, choose Join an invite link, and paste the complete shared URL.

The privacy policy is linked inside settings and at https://astrapool.darrinm.com/privacy.html. The game supports iPhone and iPad in portrait and landscape.
