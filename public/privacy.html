Astra Pool

Privacy Policy

Last updated · September 15, 2026

Astra Pool is a pool game created by Darrin Massena. This policy covers the Astra Pool website and iOS app.

No account, advertising, or tracking

You do not need an account to play. Astra Pool does not display ads, sell personal information, use advertising identifiers, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information on your device

The game stores preferences and completed-rack personal bests on your device. Online play also uses a randomly generated reconnect credential so you can keep your seat after a connection interruption. Replay recordings are held in memory. Local settings and records are not an account or a public profile.

Gameplay statistics

When connected to the internet, the game sends usage statistics to our service: a random identifier for each rack, game mode, computer difficulty, starting and current settings, shot count, duration, score, outcome, and whether the rack finished or ended early. Settings are recorded as fixed categories such as room, ball collection, sound preference, and input/device category.

We use these records to understand how the game is used and improve it. The analytics database does not store player names, email addresses, IP addresses, room links, reconnect credentials, or ball positions. A rack identifier identifies one game, not a visitor across games. Records are retained until deleted by the service administrator; there is no automatic analytics retention cutoff.

Private online games

Online matches transmit the table state, aiming and shot data, game results, and connection status to our server and the other player in your room. The server stores room state and a hash of each player's reconnect credential. Anyone you share an invitation with may be able to join that room; do not post private invitations publicly.

Rooms expire after approximately 24 hours without activity. Cleanup may be delayed while pending anonymous game statistics are delivered; failed analytics deliveries are discarded after their retry deadline. The game has no player chat or public user-content feed.

Service providers and network information

Cloudflare hosts the website, online rooms, and gameplay analytics. Network requests necessarily expose information such as your IP address to the hosting service. The analytics endpoint uses the connecting IP address to limit abusive traffic without saving it in the gameplay database. Hosting providers may process operational and security logs under their own policies.

Apple distributes the iOS app and operates App Store and TestFlight services. Apple may provide crash reports or testing feedback according to your Apple settings and the terms of those services. External links and the system share sheet are handled by the service or app you choose.

Support

If you contact us or report a problem, we receive the information you provide. GitHub issues are public; avoid including private information in an issue. Use the repository's issue tracker for non-sensitive bug reports or feature requests.

Your choices

Local games, computer opponents, and Free Play in the iOS app work offline. Online matches require an internet connection. You can remove locally stored settings and records by clearing the website's data or deleting the iOS app and its data. Because gameplay analytics do not identify a person, we may be unable to associate a particular record with you.

Children

Astra Pool does not request children's names, contact details, or accounts, and is not designed as a service specifically for children. The same limited gameplay-statistics collection described above applies to all players.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The published policy will show its updated date. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Astra Pool repository; please do not post personal information publicly.
